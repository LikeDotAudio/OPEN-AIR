## Operational Mandates
- **Temporary Scripts**: All temporary Python scripts, scratchpads, or task-runners generated by the CLI MUST be stored in `.gemini/TempScripts/`. No temporary files are permitted in the project root.

## Custom Commands
The project-specific commands located in `.gemini/commands/*.toml` are automatically loaded by the Gemini CLI. These include:
- `Audit*` (Architecture, ClassObjects, Comments, etc.): For codebase integrity checks.
- `FixBug`: Elite bug-fixing pipeline.
- `UltraFolder`: Structural architect for oa* module realignment.
- `Update*` (Debug, Documentation, Git, etc.): For maintenance tasks.

## Ignored Files and Folders
The following patterns are excluded from functional analysis and context, as defined in `.geminiignore`:

- `*.log`
- `*.zip`
- `*.pyc`
- `/__pycache__/`
- `.crawler/`
- `.git/` (Version control)
- `.vscode/` (IDE settings)
- oaData*/ (All Open-Air Data directories)
- oaDataLogs/Audits/
- oaDataCache/
- oaDataLogs/
- oaDataSplinks/
- oaDataRunningFiles/
- .pytest_cache/
