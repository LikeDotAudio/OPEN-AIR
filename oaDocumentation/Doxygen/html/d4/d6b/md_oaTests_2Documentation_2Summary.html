🧪 oaTests: The Validation & Maintenance Engine

The Narrative Purpose

In a complex ecosystem like OPEN-AIR, trust is earned through continuous validation. oaTests is the guardian of system integrity, providing a comprehensive suite of automated tests, forensic audits, and maintenance utilities to ensure the software remains robust under all conditions.

It serves as the final arbiter of correctness before a build is deemed ready for production, offering a high-fidelity Textual TUI that centralizes status monitoring, log analysis, and system cleanup.

Why It’s Essential

  1. Automated Validation: Executes F.I.R.S.T. principle tests across all subsystems to prevent regressions.
  2. Forensic Audits: Runs deep structural checks on the codebase, ensuring compliance with the 12-subfolder standard and architectural boundaries.
  3. System Hygiene: Provides "One-Click" cleanup for logs, cache, and large forensic datasets to keep the environment lean.
  4. Real-time Observability: Monitors High Availability (HA) roles and system metrics (CPU, RAM) to provide a snapshot of environmental health.

In essence, oaTests is the proof of performance that allows OPEN-AIR to operate with industrial-grade reliability.
