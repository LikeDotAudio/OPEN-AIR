WinkButtonToggler (<tt>window.WinkButtonToggler</tt>)

Web mirror of oaGuiElements/Core/buttons/button_wink_toggler — type **_WinkButtonToggler**.

A radio/multi group of "wink" (shutter) buttons — reuses OcaWinkButton per option; the selected option is held OPEN (lit), the others closed. Selection semantics match ButtonToggler:

  • selection_mode: radio (default) | multi; Allow_Null lets a radio click clear
  • options: array or dict with per-option color
  • group shape_type, radius, shutter_color, bezel_color, open_speed, close_speed, width, height, layout_columns

Routed in frameLayout/WidgetFactory.jsx (toggler branch: wink + toggler).
