Bug Report: Invisible XY Graphs and Graphing Instantiation Errors

Issue

  1. XY Graphs (GraphPlotter) were not rendering axes or data, appearing empty/black.
  2. Multiple graphing aliases (DynamicGraph, DynamicBarGraph) threw "Unknown functional widget" errors.

Root Cause Analysis

  1. Visibility Logic: GraphPatinaMixin was hiding the Matplotlib figure and axis patches (set_visible(False)) when "transparent" or "match_theme" backgrounds were used. This prevented the fast-blit engine from capturing a valid background fabric, making the plot invisible.
  2. Missing Mappings: The centralized factory_mapping.py lacked entries for DynamicGraph and DynamicBarGraph, causing the layout engine to fail during instantiation.
  3. Initialization Race: The blit engine requires at least one full draw to capture its background buffer. If data arrived before the first draw, or if patina sync occurred after the initial build, the buffer remained uninitialized.

Resolutions

  1. Graph Transparency: Updated GraphPatinaMixin to use set_alpha(0.0) instead of set_visible(False). This keeps the patches logically active for the blit engine while visually transparent.
  2. Factory Expansion: Added DynamicGraph, DynamicBarGraph, and other aliases to oaGui/Core/factory/Core/factory_mapping.py.
  3. Forced Draw: Added a forced initial redraw (self._force_redraw = True) during GraphPlotter initialization to ensure the blit cache is primed regardless of background sync timing.

Files Modified

  • oaGuiElements/Core/graphing/graphing/Core/graph_patina_mixin.py
  • oaGuiElements/Core/graphing/graphing/dynamic_graph.py
  • oaGui/Core/factory/Core/factory_mapping.py

Verification Evidence

The combination of alpha-based transparency and blit cache priming ensures that the graph remains visible and functional under all background configurations. The factory mapping fix was verified by the absence of "Unknown functional widget" errors in the latest logs.
