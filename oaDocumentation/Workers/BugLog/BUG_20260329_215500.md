# Bug Report: BUG_20260329_215500

## Issue
The ProtocolRouter was emitting high-velocity `[INBOUND]` and `[OUTBOUND]` debug logs to the console even when `LOCAL_DEBUG` was disabled. This caused console flooding and performance overhead during normal operation.

## Root Cause
Several `router_logger.debug()` calls in `oaComBroker/Core/protocol_router/ingest.py` and `oaComBroker/Core/protocol_router/dispatch.py` were not wrapped in `if LOCAL_DEBUG:` gates.

## Resolution
1.  **Ingest Gating**: Wrapped the primary `[INBOUND]` log in `oaComBroker/Core/protocol_router/ingest.py` with `if LOCAL_DEBUG:`.
2.  **Dispatch Gating**: Wrapped all protocol-specific `[OUTBOUND]` logs (MQTT, OSC, MIDI, SNMP) in `oaComBroker/Core/protocol_router/dispatch.py` with `if LOCAL_DEBUG:`.
3.  **Standard Compliance**: Ensured that the logs now correctly follow the project's "Zero-Cost Gates" architectural mandate.

## Verification
1.  Verified that `oaComBroker/Core/protocol_router/constants.py` has `LOCAL_DEBUG = False`.
2.  Verified that the identified log lines are now properly conditional.
