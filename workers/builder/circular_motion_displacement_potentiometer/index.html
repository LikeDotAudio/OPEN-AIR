CMPD circular motion displacement potentiometer - Anthony@Kuzub.com

Left-Drag: Adjust Depth (Near=0, Far=100)

Right-Drag: Adjust Intensity (Knob)

Alt + Left / Middle-Drag: Move Azimuth

Scroll: Adjust Intensity | Ctrl + Scroll: Rotate Widget

Group Controls:
• Click 👁️: Toggle Vis
• Dbl-Click 👁️: Solo
• Click Name: Select
• Dbl-Click Name: Rename
• Mid-Drag Name: Rotate
